# Policy-specific assessment · display 2.4.2

23 November 2026. Population remains 1,000; behavioral rules and initial-state schema remain 2.4.0. This update adds an observation layer, not a policy-effect multiplier.

## Three stages and time

A policy selector controls output and outcome. Impact is always the reform scenario or combined package, not an estimate of an individual tool's causal contribution. Three headlines remain together; wide screens show aligned charts, while narrow screens show the selected stage's larger chart. Impact's headline and chart display the paired difference in percentage points, with underlying rates shown precisely beneath. A shared review-month slider follows the latest month by default. Completion changes Current to Final. Reviewing earlier months does not rerun the simulation.

Expanded details compare Before reform (immediately before formal adoption), Current/Final reform, and the same-month baseline. Stock/condition measures are snapshots. Output always uses actual activity in a trailing 12-month window, including the reconstructed pre-policy year where necessary during early months. Its starting value is the reconstructed year's activity, not an opening stock. The impact-window control affects only impact, whose post-adoption rate can use the past 12 months or since adoption. The impact starting value separately describes the reconstructed pre-policy year and is labeled accordingly.

The observation recorder is outside simulation state. It accumulates actual recorded processes and snapshots each month. The reconstructed year's observation archive is deterministic, cached separately, and recreated by initial-state validation. It does not alter agents, funds, queues, cases, random streams or imported starting societies. Identical snapshots against the previously published engine are explicitly checked.

Output is process delivery, not a success score. Teaching counts episodes, not unique people or successful persuasion. Notices measure additional-reform communications, not every existing legal obligation. Disclosure currently models delivered implementation work and record quality rather than countable government documents; no documents or publications are fabricated for this report. Several tools retain estimated capability outcomes, explicitly labeled. These diagnostics do not become observed success rates merely because they are plotted.

A staffing transfer does not include entry to or exit from the public service. Screenings and investigations count completed processing stages, including a completed screening that screens out a file; they do not imply guilt. Reported threats, reports and files do not measure all hidden misconduct. General case/service totals remain available in expandable system context, not as universal policy conditions.

## Interpretations and evidence links

The predefined catalog in reporting.js handles more/less/equal delivery, absent delivery, institutional improvement/deterioration, activity without institutional change, institutional change with limited conduct change, positive/negative package impacts, and insufficient observations. Direction is indicator-specific. The zero/limited comparison uses displayed numerical precision, not a statistical significance threshold. Statements describe the model and never automatically assert causation.

References are drawn from real tracked records, not generated anecdotes. Direct policy events match their P-policy tracking ID; case references are explicitly contextual and limited to the corresponding process. Salient macro/political events can accompany package impact. Events after the review month and unrelated policies are excluded. Actual implementation transitions, authorized funding changes and suspensions rank above routine reviews. At most two references are inline; further connected developments expand below. References open a tracked-record panel without leaving the assessment and highlight participating agents. Earlier developments may remain relevant to a current result; each reference carries its actual month.

A bounded observation evidence index retains at most 400 records, protecting per-policy consequential records from live-feed truncation. The full process log remains in completed exports. Event links describe connected developments; chronology alone is not labeled as the cause of the measured difference.
