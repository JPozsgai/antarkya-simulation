Assumptions, limitations and implementation gaps

This is a public limitation register, not a queue of silently authorized behavioral changes. A documented gap is not repaired by describing it. Some mechanisms have executable pathways but remain partial or uncalibrated; their exact boundaries are listed here and beside their equations.

Audit conclusions and next research needs

The code constructs real numerical agent profiles and uses their evolving values in choices and policy exposure. It also contains modeled money/staff constraints, institutional capacity, information and feedback pathways. These existence claims are inspectable; they do not establish that the priors are realistic. Membership composition, normalized resources/costs, nine-dimensional consequence vectors, uptake/retention parameters and most outcome diagnostics require sensitivity analysis and calibration.

The agreed national policy architecture is represented through simplified functional nodes and limited legal-scope/descriptor mappings, rather than 120 complete country–policy legal packages. Several practical, political and strategic processes remain simplified or absent. No empirical calibration, Peru pilot validation, systematic uncertainty analysis or actual Android performance certification is supplied by this provisional release.

Original v1 behavior that was expressly superseded is distinguished from a missing v2 process in the decision crosswalk. When a reporting definition has aspirational wording, the technical rules identify the actual proxy used. Outputs, capability diagnostics, observed rolling outcomes and package-level impact must not be merged into one success score.
