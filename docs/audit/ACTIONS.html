They are subjective modeled consequence scores, not monetary transfers or empirical welfare estimates. Monetary costs/benefits and learned accountability costs are further transformed in choose; practical feasibility and completion are handled separately. A numeric action payoff is an engineering prior; expressions such as need or bribe are computed in the linked source.
