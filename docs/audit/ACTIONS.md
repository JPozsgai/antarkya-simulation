These are **all** explicit `this.effects(social, economic, political)` calls in the active engine. Each vector has beneficiary order `[self, group, public]`; flattening social then economic then political matches the nine motive weights. They are subjective modeled consequence scores, not monetary transfers or empirical welfare estimates. Monetary costs/benefits and learned accountability costs are further transformed in `choose`; practical feasibility and completion are handled separately. A numeric action payoff is an engineering prior; expressions such as `need` or `bribe` are computed in the linked source. Dynamic option construction and branch conditions remain inspectable in full code.
