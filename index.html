Setup

Reforms and starting mobilization

Improve existing arrangements. Starting mobilization sets a share of estimated additional requirements; it is not a fresh monthly funding lottery. Resources can later rise or fall through actor decisions.

Setup and recurring costs accumulate. Reforms share finite staff time with ordinary services and cases.

Estimated country conditions and sources

World Bank 2025 observations. WGI values are revised 0–100 governance descriptors, not corruption or sanction probabilities. Regulatory quality is an incomplete proxy for business conditions; GDP per capita is not personal income.

Political proxies, cultural assumptions and allocation

Political settings use dated Freedom House proxies rescaled to 0–100: civil liberties (2026), electoral process and pluralism/participation (2025). Cultural conditions remain unmeasured neutral assumptions of 50; allocation is estimated. All values can be overridden.

    Comparisons and reproducibility

    By default, Run and Run again use fresh random events. Check exact replay to reproduce the seed shown above. Pause/continue and Step retain the active run’s seed.

    The reform and no-new-reform baseline start from the same exact society. Repetitions also retain that society; only subsequent random events vary. The visible map shows the first reform run. Changing policy selection or its funding does not regenerate the initial people.

    How to read the assessment

    Inputs: formal provisions, intended scope and resources mobilized against requirements.

    Outputs: actual activities, coverage and fidelity.

    Outcomes: the measure-specific practical institutional change.

    Impact: changes in completed conduct, burdens, diversion and services versus the paired baseline.

    “Not assessable” means insufficient applicable observations, not zero corruption or full success. Diagnostics do not change the simulated society or consume its random events.
