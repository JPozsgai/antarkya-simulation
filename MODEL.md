# Antarkya v2.2 — operational model

23 November 2026 · engine and rules 2.2.0

This is an exploratory 1,000-person corruption and anti-corruption model. It combines sourced country descriptors and simplified institutional architecture with explicit provisional behavioral, resource and coverage assumptions. Engineering verification does not establish a calibrated national forecast.

The current [policy influence and individual adaptation rules](docs/POLICY-INFLUENCE.md) are implemented in `adaptation.js` and actual event hooks in `model.js`. Agents now store numerical receptiveness traits, bounded policy/component memories, diminishing response caps and wear-off.

The executable rule set is [docs/NUMERICAL-PROPOSAL.md](docs/NUMERICAL-PROPOSAL.md). The earlier proposal is retained as [the v0.1 archive](docs/NUMERICAL-PROPOSAL-v0.1.md). [Action-specific consequence matrices](docs/ACTION-CONSEQUENCES.md) and [audit-to-code traceability](docs/CAUSAL-AUDIT.md) identify the numerical rules, implementing methods and controlled checks.

## Behavior and organizations

Every person has nine social/economic/political × self/group/public motive attachments. Intentional decisions maximize subjective utility within TPB: action-specific consequences, perceived effort, reference expectations, residual capability and occasion-specific uncertainty. All roles use this mechanism. The nine consequence dimensions are separately specified for each action, rather than generated by fixed multiples of a generic benefit.

Motivational attachments and openness/learning/retention traits evolve slowly through experience; professional skill changes through performed work. Expected lawful income is learned from actual receipts.

People maintain distinct memberships, support, beliefs, financial resources and directional relationship memories. Affinity and trusted access affect whom they encounter; peer sharing depends on the receiver's trust in the sender. Organizational skill and actual staff competence jointly determine productive capacity. Role changes preserve the person, history, money, home and ties.

Role-linked expectations become easier to satisfy through accessed reactions from relevant references. Merely counting repeated behavior does not create an institution. Coercion does not turn a victim into a corrupt collaborator or a mutually supportive network member.

## Resources and implementation

Money moves through conserved accounts. Public revenue, lawful pay, consumption, service queues and reform allocations evolve within the existing economic variables; national GDP and background indices remain fixed.

Selected reforms make de jure changes at the user's persistent initial mobilization rate. Setup, additional funding and finite staff time determine actual delivery. Leaders can implement, selectively comply, resist or redirect. Political actors set funding priorities, local leaders set implementation priorities, and organizations allocate staff between reform, routine and case work. Source-menu order is not a workload priority.

The twelve tools operate through actual procedures: declarations change verifiability used in audits; conflicts can prevent improper exchanges; trained scrutiny changes evidence acquisition; penalties change actual collection; remuneration changes actual receipts and subjective financial security; staff decisions change subsequent productive capacity. No selected checkbox supplies an automatic integrity bonus.

Experienced service reliability and waiting update access expectations and organizational/political support. Six-month resource reviews use observed delivery, waiting, support and resistance, subject to affordability and authorized consent. This supplies collective feedback without adding new macroeconomic variables.

## Country baselines and procedures

The ten profiles retain sourced GDP PPP, government effectiveness, rule of law and regulatory-quality descriptors, plus institutional evidence. Numeric political/cultural estimates, role composition, allocation and costs remain visible assumptions.

Legal authority is function-specific and operative. Individual coverage uses fixed reference draws; trained capacity cannot substitute for missing authority. Initial .55 and adopted .85 scope values are **common exploratory coverage priors**, not country effectiveness estimates. Country evidence tags indicating an instrument, a scope gap or an unscoped tool are not converted to made-up success probabilities.

Configured country architecture changes screening, specialist/general referral, investigation, prosecution, administrative determination and court routes. Nigerian/Indonesian/Chinese specialist bodies can investigate qualifying covered matters. Indian complaint referral and Peruvian political-finance intake use distinct functions. South African civil recovery remains separate from criminal conviction. Chinese supervision-linked administrative action and personnel appointments retain their own routes. Other distributed architectures retain separate prevention, investigation, audit and court functions. Regional intake and handoffs consume actual time, resources and delay. Detailed real-world jurisdiction and financial calibration remain incomplete.

Editors and journalists belong to existing organizational categories. Recent actual funding dependence and editorial independence affect publication choices. Information travels through geographically constrained exposure and weighted directional ties. Claims, proof, corrections, dismissal and model truth remain separate.

## Readouts and interface

The analytical plot uses class-reference quadrants, role colors and conduct shapes. Recent repeated corrupt participation fixes distance from the integrity center. Affinity/cooperation changes direction and relative grouping without changing membership or geographical encounters. No baseline distribution is overlaid.

The last 24 meaningful integrity opportunities support bounded participation rates and four compact current-class cards, alongside paired baseline differences. Lifetime conduct remains separate. A triangle follows one qualifying act; recovery requires six months plus three meaningful integrity choices. Inactivity cannot clear recent conduct.

Major events are the default live feed: authority changes, significant existing fiscal/service/pay conditions, consolidation/erosion of expectations, broad disclosures and evidence of consequential coordinated abuse. Dense networks alone do not establish capture. The full process log and case counts remain available in details/exports.

Institutional diagnostics use applicable scope and capacity, actual observations where available, and fixed read-only comparison situations. Education uses the expected retained knowledge capability across all 1,000 people and cannot rise merely through formal adoption. Inputs, outputs, measure-specific outcomes and joint package impact remain distinct. Icons, plot coordinates, assessment scores and event-reporting thresholds do not directly determine agents' behavior.

## Reproducibility and validation boundary

The initial society is shared exactly across branches and repetitions. Parameter-independent initial random streams keep unrelated personal characteristics stable in controlled comparisons. Event seeds vary subsequent events; exact replay intentionally reproduces the same trajectory. Display controls, rendering pace and diagnostics consume no event randomness.

The regression, causal, visualization and stress suites are described in [docs/VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md). They test engineering consistency and specific causal pathways. They do not establish empirical effect sizes, comprehensive country-law fidelity, robust sensitivity results or Android-browser performance.

Rules version 2.2.0 deliberately rejects older saved initial-state formats; those trajectories cannot be relabeled as results of the new rules. Recreate a scenario from its settings.
